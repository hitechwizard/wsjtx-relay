Examples: WSJT-X Relay as the Middle Man

Configure WSJT-X Relay to listen on 2237, then relay to multiple WSJT-X-aware applications running on different local ports.

Traffic Flow

Communication is bidirectional: packets from WSJT-X flow through Relay to downstream apps, and reply packets (for example from GridTracker) are forwarded back to WSJT-X.

Callsign and Gridsquare come from WSJT-X General Settings tab.

If the WSJT-X configration name is in [callsign]@[park id] format, WSJT-X Relay will populate My Park as well.

2) WSJT-X Relay settings

WSJT-X Relay listens on 2237 and relays to: 2238 (WRL CAT Control), 2239 (GridTracker), and 2240 (openHamclock).

3) WRL CAT Control

Configure WRL CAT Control to listen for WSJT-X packets on 2238.

4) GridTracker
